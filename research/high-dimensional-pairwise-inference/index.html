Research / High-dimensional pairwise inference

Doctoral research · Manuscript 02

Discovering what matters—and quantifying it—from pairwise data

A dataset with N observations can generate order N² comparisons. In high dimensions, a marginal screen can miss variables that become important only after other variables are fitted. This project builds one pipeline for discovery, estimation, and simultaneous uncertainty reporting.

Overview

This work develops a scalable framework for identifying variables and reporting simultaneous coefficient intervals in high-dimensional models whose loss is defined over pairs of observations. Instead of enumerating all quadratic-many pairs, it builds a balanced objective from logarithmically many random matchings. A fully corrective hard-thresholding pursuit repeatedly proposes variables using residual gradients, jointly refits supports, and retains a sparse candidate set, allowing variables with weak marginal signals to enter later. Warm-started, preconditioned pairwise stochastic gradient descent then cleans and refits the selected model. Sampled Hessians and Hájek placement values estimate uncertainty, while a dependence-aware Gaussian maximum calibrates simultaneous intervals. The theory gives exact-support recovery and an oracle limit under fixed-separation, restricted-curvature, and growth conditions, with every selection or computation failure counted as noncoverage. Frozen simulations cover 180 design cells and 360,000 route-level records, including rank-regression and bipartite-ranking examples, and expose finite-sample limits under heavy contamination, class imbalance, and sparsity caps.

Method

An end-to-end pairwise pipeline

Balanced pair sampling

Build the discovery objective from logarithmically many random maximal matchings.

Residual-gradient hard thresholding

Propose variables after each joint refit, allowing a weak marginal signal to enter once related variables are fitted.

Pair-SGD cleaning and refitting

Warm-started, preconditioned stochastic optimization fits and cleans the candidate, then refits the selected support with fresh streams.

Sampled Hájek covariance

Estimate dependence-aware uncertainty without enumerating the complete pair population.

Simultaneous intervals

Calibrate a Gaussian maximum on the recovered support; every non-reportable failure is strict noncoverage.

Technical details

The balanced objective uses R_N=ceil(log(p∨e)) random matchings, producing B_N of order N log p balanced pairs. For the difference-form rank and AUC examples, endpoint aggregation computes each full-p residual gradient in O(Np + B_N) work rather than enumerating N² pairs.

The inferential claim is unconditional oracleization after asymptotically exact support recovery. It is not conditional validity after an arbitrary selection event.

Why residual iteration matters

At normalized marginal gap .048 with s=20, a one-shot marginal screen contained the full support in 0 of 500 replications and captured 4.3 of 20 signals on average. The iterative residual pipeline contained the full support in all 500 replications.

Constructed invisible coordinate. One-shot screening missed the designated coordinate in 2,000 of 2,000 replications; the full pipeline recovered it in 2,000 of 2,000 on both pruning routes. This is a deliberately constructed worst case, not typical behavior.

Failure analysis

Where does the pipeline break?

Pooled averages are not allowed to hide difficult cells.

Weak studentized separation

Three worst unknown-s boundary cells cover .811–.830; recovery alone caps the strict event at .890–.909.

Root nonlinearity

Under heavy contamination, the exact root and exhaustive sandwich already under-cover; optimizer error is not the main mechanism.

Class imbalance

The practical break appears at positive-class prevalence at or below .10, roughly 100 or fewer positives at the tested tolerances.

Loose sparsity cap

At s=20, raising K from 2s to 3s lowers unknown-s recovery .983→.823 and strict coverage .945→.780.

Marginal screening

One-shot screens can miss jointly important variables even when the iterative residual procedure recovers them.

Global null

The unknown-s route returns an empty model in 399/400 replications for each instance, with one spurious selection and zero crashes.
