Causal interpretation and clinical context

Causal challenges in SMA

Disease stage and prior response
Age and disease duration influence treatment response in SMA.1 A baseline score recorded after treatment starts may already reflect earlier response.
Treatment route and availability
Spinal deformity can complicate intrathecal nusinersen; risdiplam is oral and became available later.2, 3 Disease burden and access can therefore influence treatment choice as well as outcomes.
Who remains under observation
Procedure burden or perceived loss of benefit may prompt switching.4 Excluding post-switch scores can select patients by clinical course; treatment weights alone do not resolve that selection.

These clinical mechanisms are described in published SMA literature; they are not findings about prescribing decisions in this registry.

Weighting can address measured treatment-group differences, while mixed models account for repeated assessments. Neither establishes causal identification or removes bias from unmeasured confounding, selective follow-up or treatment switching.

Results, cohort details and study materials are withheld pending publication and approval for public release.
