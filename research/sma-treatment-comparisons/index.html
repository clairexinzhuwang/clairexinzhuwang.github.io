Clinical research / SMA treatment comparisons

Motor outcomes during nusinersen and risdiplam treatment in spinal muscular atrophy: an APNCR registry study

Advisors: Abdus S. Wahed (University of Rochester); Divya Jayaraman (Department of Neurology, Columbia University).

I designed the HFMSE and RULM comparison around three registry constraints: on-treatment baselines, unequal treatment groups and irregular follow-up.

Methods overview · Manuscript and patient data remain unreleased, pending advisor approval.

Where the comparison begins

Conditions for causal interpretation
  • Treatment assignment: overlap weighting addresses measured differences; unmeasured severity or access to care may still confound the comparison.
  • Identification: well-defined strategies, consistency, exchangeability, positivity and suitable observation assumptions are required. These diagrams do not establish an identified direct effect.
  • Outcome observation: eligible complete postbaseline scores enter fitting under conditional missing at random. Neither weighting nor a random intercept guarantees unbiased follow-up.

Fitting weights ≠ the reference population

Model specification and follow-up
Four models, one comparison target per outcome
Fixed effectsUnweightedOverlap weighted
Treatment onlyM1M2
Treatment + time + treatment × time + baseline covariatesM3M4

All models have a patient random intercept and use the same eligible records. Baseline covariates: outcome score, age, sex, SMA type, SMN2 category, treatment-start year and treatment duration before baseline.

In weighted models, each patient’s overlap weight multiplies the joint log likelihood after integrating out the random intercept. Final fitted contrasts are time-averaged within individual baseline-to-endpoint windows, then averaged equally across the full baseline reference cohort.

Outcome-specific baselines and a common treatment-duration cap define the windows, ending earlier at switching, death or the data cutoff. Bootstrap resampling is within treatment groups; numerical failures and zero random-intercept variance fits are recorded.
