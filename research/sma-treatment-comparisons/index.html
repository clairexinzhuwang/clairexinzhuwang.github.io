Clinical research / SMA treatment comparisons

Motor outcomes during nusinersen and risdiplam treatment in spinal muscular atrophy: an APNCR registry study

Advisors: Abdus S. Wahed (University of Rochester); Divya Jayaraman (Department of Neurology, Columbia University).

I developed an overlap-weighted longitudinal analysis of HFMSE and RULM, separating patient-level estimation from the population and time window defining the comparison.

Methods overview · Manuscript and patient data remain unreleased, pending advisor approval.

Four models, one target per outcome

Causal scope: an on-treatment baseline

Baseline already reflects treatment exposure. The adjusted comparison concerns subsequent function given baseline status and treatment history, rather than total benefit from treatment initiation.

Technical specification

All models have a patient random intercept and use the same eligible records within each outcome. Baseline covariates: outcome score, age, sex, SMA type, SMN2 category, treatment-start year and treatment duration before baseline.

The interaction term is absent in treatment-only models. Numerical failures and zero random-intercept variance fits are recorded in the bootstrap.

Conditions for causal interpretation
  • Assignment: overlap weighting addresses measured differences; unmeasured severity or access to care may still confound the comparison.
  • Support: standardizing to the full baseline reference cohort, including baseline-only patients, requires greater model reliance where overlap or follow-up is limited.
  • Observation: eligible complete postbaseline scores enter fitting under conditional missing at random. Treatment weights do not correct selective follow-up.
  • Identification: well-defined strategies, consistency, exchangeability, positivity and suitable observation assumptions remain necessary. The analysis does not establish a treatment-initiation effect or an identified direct effect.
