Research practice

Built to be inspected

Inference matches the implemented estimator

The reported object is the estimator returned by the implemented procedure—not an ideal exact optimizer substituted after the fact.

Failures remain in the denominator

Selection, optimization, covariance, calibration, and empty-selection failures are recorded rather than silently removed from operating-characteristic summaries.

Formal panels and later sensitivities stay distinct

Correlated-design and contamination panels are prespecified components of the 180-cell formal grid. Growing-minibatch and sample-splitting studies are labeled separately as additional stress or sensitivity analyses.

Claims stop at the stated scope

Each project page states the assumptions and the regimes that need new analysis, including dependence, growing dimension, approximate sparsity, and adaptive stopping.
