Manuscript in preparation

High-dimensional inference for U-statistic models

Thesis advisors: Brent A. Johnson (University of Rochester) and Ollivier Hyrien (Affiliate Professor, University of Washington, Seattle).

I study confidence intervals for prespecified coefficients when predictors outnumber observations. The target is the population U-statistic loss minimizer; exact recovery of every nonzero coefficient is not required.

Simulation findingsScope

Simulation findings

Joint coverage asks whether all prespecified intervals contain their population coefficients.

Study context and interpretation

Reported pairwise rank-regression simulations include correlated predictors, heavy-tailed errors and weak marginal signals. The results are transcribed from the supplied study summary, not an independent rerun.

Each setting uses 50 or 100 simulated datasets. Small differences should not be overinterpreted; observed 100% coverage is not exact calibration. Monte Carlo uncertainty intervals are not shown.

Theory and scope

The theory gives conditions for asymptotically valid marginal and simultaneous inference for a fixed target set. It covers U-statistic criteria of fixed order; the current experiments concern pairwise rank regression.

Independent observations and the stated regularity, bias and numerical-accuracy conditions are required. The results do not establish a finite-sample guarantee, a standalone speedup or superiority over published methods.
