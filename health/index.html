Observational health research

Causal inference for SMA treatment comparisons

Study design, confounding, and the limits of real-world evidence.

SMA · 2025–present

Longitudinal motor outcomes in spinal muscular atrophy

My work applies causal-inference methods to observational comparisons of nusinersen and risdiplam. I focus on defining comparable patient groups, adjusting for measured confounding, and assessing how incomplete follow-up and modeling assumptions affect interpretation of motor-function change.

The current analysis uses an on-treatment baseline and longitudinal outcomes. It estimates an adjusted between-group comparison; the assumptions needed to interpret it as a causal treatment effect have not been established.

Ongoing collaborative research · Unpublished manuscript · Public release subject to advisor approval

Design and overlap
Align eligibility, baseline, and follow-up; use propensity-score overlap weighting and assess covariate balance among both baseline participants and actual follow-up contributors.
Longitudinal analysis
Model motor-function change with repeated observations and patient-level resampling; examine the influence of observation timing and model specification.
Sensitivity
Assess sensitivity to unmeasured confounding using observed-covariate benchmarks, alongside incomplete-follow-up and data-quality audits.

Participant allocation and final dose selection

Research on efficacy–toxicity tradeoffs in two-agent dose finding.

Ongoing research

Bayesian Optimization for Dose Finding with Two Agents: Participant Allocation and Final Selection

I conceived the study, adapted a constrained knowledge-gradient method, developed its computational evaluator, implemented and ran the simulations, and drafted the manuscript under Tanzy Love’s supervision.

The work examines participant allocation and final dose selection under efficacy and toxicity constraints. It evaluates how optimization decisions affect different operating characteristics rather than assuming a single measure captures performance.

Bayesian optimization · Gaussian processes · Constrained knowledge gradient · Simulation design
