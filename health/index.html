When the right result is to narrow the claim

SMA · 2025–present

Comparative-evidence redesign

An initial extract contained 318 patients, but only 13 had aligned pretreatment and one-year motor-function measurements. I helped reframe the final report as feasibility and hypothesis-generating evidence rather than a claim of superiority, inferiority, or equivalence.

The redesigned plan separated first-line initiation from switch-versus-continue questions and made time zero, eligibility, estimands, confounding, missingness, and evidence thresholds explicit.

Statistical learning in health data

The finite-compute project includes a 30-day readmission ranking analysis with 41,984 fitting observations and 143.9 million possible cross-class pairs. The implemented fit evaluates 644,608 pairs; the single-run standard errors are compared with 300 stratified bootstrap refits. This comparison measures standard-error agreement, not coverage or predictive superiority.
