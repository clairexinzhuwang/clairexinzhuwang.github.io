Regression · Model evaluation

Smartphone-image hemoglobin prediction

I fitted regression models and compared image-region strategies against laboratory measurements. On the combined test set, pooling three image regions gave a mean absolute error of 1.26 g/dL versus 1.33 g/dL for the best region-specific model. Validation in new infants remains necessary.
