Featured project 01 · Experimentation

Email Campaign Experiment

A reproducible intention-to-treat analysis of a public 64,000-customer, three-arm randomized campaign. The analysis uses an explicitly stated comparison plan; the recommendation states what the data cannot answer.

64,000 customers

Decision

Advance Mens E-Mail as the candidate for a monitored rollout. It produced the largest observed two-week lift across visits, conversions, and spend, but a profit decision still needs campaign cost, margin, deliverability, and unsubscribe guardrails. The single planned newbie check did not support a targeting rule.

Featured project 02 · Recommender evaluation

Training-Budget Trade-offs in a Standard Recommender

An independent MovieLens benchmark using conventional BPR-SGD. It evaluates how epochs and sampled negatives change held-out ranking quality, descriptive cross-seed agreement, and measured CPU training time.

Data
100,836 ratings
Catalog
9,742 movies
Runs
4 settings × 3 seeds
Metric
5 epochs · 1 negative per positive 239,855 updates 0.385–0.391 across seeds
Decision

Use the 5-epoch, 1-negative setting for routine iteration: it trained in 4.57 seconds with NDCG@10 of 0.388 and the strongest mean top-10 agreement. Use the 5-epoch, 3-negative setting for a quality-focused final run when a +0.016 NDCG@10 and +2.08 percentage-point HR@10 gain justify 2.88× the training time. This is a result for this development benchmark, not a universal stopping rule.
